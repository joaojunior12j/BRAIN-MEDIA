Brain Media

A publishing tool for creators who manage their own short-video channels.

Brain Media helps creators connect their own TikTok and Instagram accounts and publish short videos they produced. Creators choose the caption, select the privacy level offered by each platform, and confirm every post before it goes live.

We do not post anything without the creator's explicit confirmation, and we do not collect followers, messages, or any data beyond what is strictly needed to publish on behalf of the authenticated account.
