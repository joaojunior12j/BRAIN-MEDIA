Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what Brain Media ("we", "the app") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By connecting an account to Brain Media, you agree to the terms below.

Information we collect

When you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, we receive:

  • A platform-issued access token that authorizes Brain Media to publish on your behalf.
  • Basic profile information from the platform (display name, avatar, and the platform's internal user ID) so you can confirm which account is connected.

For each video you choose to publish, we receive the video file, the caption, and the privacy level you select.

What we do not collect

We do not read, collect, or store: your followers, the accounts you follow, direct messages, comments, your email on the platform, your phone number, content posted by other accounts, or any data not strictly needed to publish the videos you select.

How we use your information

We use access tokens and video submissions only to publish the specific videos you choose, with the caption you wrote and the privacy level you selected, to the account you authorized. Nothing is posted without your explicit confirmation.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept while your account is connected and are deleted when you disconnect. Videos submitted for publication and their metadata are kept only as long as needed to complete the post and to show you a record of what was published.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform you authorized (TikTok, Instagram), in order to publish the content you selected.

Your rights

You can disconnect your account at any time from inside your TikTok or Instagram settings, which immediately revokes our access. You may also request deletion of any data we hold by writing to the contact address below.
